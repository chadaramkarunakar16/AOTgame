Game Controls & Settings

Complete manual for Scout 3DMG Maneuver Gear, Titan Shifter transformation & brawl combat, Cavalry riding, and Tactical Multiplayer.
⚔️ Titan Shifter Form (Full Combat & Abilities) 九つの巨人 · TITAN FORM
Available in Titan vs Titan Arena, or anywhere in story/free-roam by pressing [T]
T
Transform / Eject: Press [T] to summon lightning & transform into your Titan Shifter. Press [T] again inside Titan to eject with a burst of steam and return to human form.
LMB
Heavy Punch & Strike: Multi-hit martial arts fist combo. Staggers enemy Titans and delivers devastating concussive blows.
RMB
Grab & Throw: Reach out to grab Titans or Scouts, lift them overhead, and fling them across the battlefield.
SPACE
Titan Jump & Leap: High-power hydraulic leap. Scale rooftops, jump across wall gaps, and create shockwaves upon landing.
F
Heavy Kick & Ground Smash: High-damage kick that breaks knee armor plates, topples towering Titans, and stomps grounded enemies.
C / CTRL
Hardened Nape Guard: Raise hardened crystal hands to cover and shield your nape from fatal strikes.
Q
Ability 1 · Crystallization / Hardening: Encase knuckles and armor in hardened crystal to boost strike damage and deflect incoming attacks.
E
Ability 2 · Primal War Cry: Release a thunderous sonic roar that stuns nearby enemy Titans and interrupts enemy attacks.
R
Ability 3 · Ground Spikes / Wall Breaker: Slam the earth to send piercing crystal spikes forward or execute a cataclysmic ground slam.
W A S D
Crippled Roll & Crawl: When knocked down or crippled, move WASD to roll and crawl out of danger.
SPACE / F
Instant Kip-Up Recovery: When knocked down, press Space or F to immediately spring back to your feet.
🕊️ Scout Regiment 3D Maneuver Gear (ODM) 立体機動装置 · HUMAN FORM
High-speed kinetic flight, vector cable grappling, and lethal nape slashes
LMB / RMB
Left & Right Anchors: Hold to fire grappling wires. Dual anchoring creates a slingshot effect around buildings.
W A S D
Movement & Aerial Steering: Run on the ground; steer swings, lean into turns, and pump kinetic momentum in mid-air.
SPACE
Gas Thruster Boost: Fire gas thrusters for sudden explosive acceleration in view direction (jump on the ground).
SHIFT
Cable Reel-In / Sprint: Rapidly reel in anchor cables to pull directly toward tether points at maximum velocity.
G
Landing Thrusters (Air Brake): Reverse thrusters that instantly kill dangerous vertical fall speed to prevent wall slam damage.
F
Blade Strike: Slashes target nape. Speeds above 30 m/s unleash Captain Levi's supersonic spin cut.
R
Blade Replacement: Swap dull or broken blades with a fresh sharp set from your scabbard (8 spare sets).
F5 / V
Camera Toggle: Switch between 3rd-person cinematic combat camera and immersive 1st-person view.
🐎 Military Steed & Cavalry Riding 騎兵 · CAVALRY REGIMENT
High-speed ground transport across Shiganshina, Wall ramparts, and outer plains
H
Mount / Dismount / Whistle: Press [H] near horse to mount; press [H] while riding to dismount; press [H] from afar to whistle horse to your side.
W A S D
Steering & Gallop: Guide your horse forward, backward, and through turns.
SHIFT
Cavalry Charge: Accelerate to full gallop speed across plains and avenues.
SPACE
Steed Jump: Leap over debris, low fences, and terrain obstacles.
SPACE / F
ODM Aerial Dismount: Jump or launch hooks while riding to leap high off the saddle straight into flight.
IMPACT
Obstacle Rebound: Galloping into solid structures triggers a physical bounce-back instead of getting stuck.
🗺️ Tactical Map, Multiplayer & Settings 戦術 · TACTICAL SYSTEMS
M
Tactical Territory Map: Open full real-time interactive map showing Wall Rose, Wall Maria, steed location, and Titans.
V
Push-to-Talk Voice Chat: Talk live with squadron friends in multiplayer lobbies.
ENTER / T
Squadron Chat: Send live text messages to squadron members.
TAB
Scoreboard: Display live scoreboard, titan slays, match scores, and ping.
ESC
Pause & Settings: Pause single-player match, resume, or quit to title.
GAMEPAD
Full Controller Support: LT/RT hooks · A boost · LB reel · X/RB slash · Y reload · B steed/use · R3 camera view.
